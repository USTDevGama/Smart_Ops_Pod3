(Made-up sample for development. Not real client or trainee data.)

We propose migrating the company's billing system from the legacy monolith to
microservices. The programme will run for six months with a total budget of $400,000.

Phase 2 (months 4-6): payments service, integrated with the CloudPay gateway.

3. Team
Three delivery teams will work in parallel: Invoicing, Payments and Platform.

4. Budget
The total budget is $400,000. A detailed breakdown will be shared after approval.

5. Timeline
All work completes within six months. Go-live is planned for the end of month six.

6. Vendors
CloudPay will be the only payment gateway. The contract is being finalised.

7. Expected benefits
Faster releases and lower maintenance effort. Benefits will be measured after go-live.
