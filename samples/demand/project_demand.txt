DEMAND: Billing platform modernisation
(Made-up sample for development. Not real client data.)

1. Project
A logistics client is moving its customer billing from one large legacy application to
services. The team is mid-project and deadlines are tight.

2. Stack
Spring Boot backend, React frontend, PostgreSQL database, Docker on a cloud platform.

3. Expectations of the person
Owns problems and tells the client early when a date will slip. Gives specific dates, costs
and names instead of general reassurance. Stays composed under pressure and keeps delivery
moving.

4. Pressure points
The invoicing release is due in ten days. The payments gateway contract is not signed yet, so
the date depends on a vendor.
